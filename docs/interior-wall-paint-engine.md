# Interior wall painting calculation — first job template

Phase B was published first: `ab1f0ecbd3adfbc6c8e89a6e90816aff96c2b005`, Netlify deploy `6ad43b08a39997a176e23a1c`, ready at `https://onestop-beta.netlify.app` on 2026-10-18. The root and `/chat` returned 200 and the unauthenticated AI API returned 401. That release did not change `main`.

The seller describes one rectangular interior wall painting job in chat. The model interprets scope, disclosures and the three dimensions in the approved plan; `shared/paintJob.js` calculates area, whole paint packages, five distinct work phases, consumables, hours, expenses and the estimate margin. An unparseable or multi-task plan still uses the previous estimator path; expansion to those jobs is pending. The rule engine does not price ceilings, trim, doors or larger repairs.

The template in AI Settings contains editable coverage, coats, waste, minor patch count, labor productivity and hourly rate, protection/preparation/cleanup consumables per wall sf, job expenses percent, sale margin, and chosen paint and patch catalog references. Defaults are **provisional estimating assumptions**, not verified performance or private supplier prices. The engine uses a catalog price only when the exact chosen product is named in the approved plan and the stored reference is usable for the job ZIP. Otherwise the material is an editable zero-dollar pending price. Public catalog prices remain labeled as online observations; phase A confirmed none by product and branch/ZIP.

The 12 × 10 × 8 ft bedroom example, walls only with no opening deduction, uses `2 × (12 + 10) × 8 = 352 sf`. Two coats, 10% waste and assumed 350 sf/gal need `352 × 2 × 1.10 / 350 = 2.2126 gal`, rounded to **3 purchasable one-gallon packages**. The controlled fixture selects the phase A public BEHR observation at USD 46.98/gal and DAP patch at USD 11.48/container. Separate protection, preparation and cleanup consumables are USD 0.08, 0.06 and 0.03 per wall sf. The five labor phases total 6.64 hours at a provisional USD 75/hr. Material USD 212.26 + labor USD 498.00 + expenses USD 35.51 = cost USD 745.77. A 25% target **margin on sale** converts to a 33.33% **markup on cost** (stored as 3333 basis points); rounding yields USD 994.34 sale and 25.0% displayed margin. Taxes and freight are not asserted.

Every saved estimate stores the rule version, inputs, template values, catalog record snapshots and the computed line prices and sources in its existing JSON document. A later settings or catalog change leaves that estimate intact. The in-chat editor lets the seller recalculate dimensions and assumptions, choose a product, or manually override quantity, description and cost. Manual overrides retain their provenance during subsequent recalculation; expenses are recomputed from the resulting material and labor costs. The line sale unit price displays enough decimals for its quantity to round back to the displayed line amount, including allocated rounding cents. The proposal and PDF read the same saved record. No database migration was needed.

To reproduce locally, run `node scripts/paint-job-browser.mjs`. It uses an isolated migrated PGlite database and a controlled AI fixture; see `evidence/paint-job/` for saved JSON, desktop/phone screenshots, EN/ES PDF downloads, browser report and extracted PDF checks. `node --env-file=<existing authorized server env file> scripts/paint-real-eval.mjs` separately tests synthetic plan/generation calls against the configured real OpenAI provider without database writes. The controlled browser run is not a live supplier or authenticated beta test.
